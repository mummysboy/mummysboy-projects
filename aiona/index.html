Who I work with

Companies growing faster than their processes.

Scale-ups past product–market fit, where the team is stretched, the tools have multiplied, and everyone has been told to use AI without a clear picture of where it actually helps.

  • Founders and leadership teams deciding what to build, what to buy, and what to ignore
  • Operations, support and sales teams carrying a lot of repeatable work
  • Product and engineering teams adding AI features customers will pay for

The approach

Small, measured steps, from first conversation to a system your team runs.

  1. 01

    Find the work

    We map where time goes and where quality slips, with the people doing the work. You get a short list of opportunities ranked by value and effort, not a deck of possibilities.

  2. 02

    Prove it

    One opportunity becomes a working pilot on your real data, measured against how it is done today. If it does not beat the baseline, we stop and say so.

  3. 03

    Build it in

    What works goes into the tools and routines the team already uses, with a clear owner, sensible guardrails, and a way to see when it drifts.

  4. 04

    Hand it over

    Your team runs it. They get the training, the documentation and a playbook for the next use case, so the capability stays when I leave.

Working together

Three ways in, depending on where you are.

AI opportunity review

A fixed-scope look across the business to find and rank where AI pays off, ending in a recommended first project and a realistic plan to deliver it.

Best for teams who know they should be doing more with AI, but not what.

Pilot to production

One high-value use case taken from idea to a working system, measured against the current way of doing it and rolled out to the team who will use it.

Best for a clear use case that needs to ship.

Ongoing advisory

A standing line to leadership on what to build, buy or ignore as the field moves, and a second pair of eyes on vendors, hires and roadmaps.

Best for leadership teams making AI decisions every month.

About

I’m Iona Hirsch.

I spent ten years inside fast-growing software companies, first running operations, then leading product teams. Most recently I led the programme that took AI from a handful of experiments to tools people use every day across support, sales and finance.

I started Aiona in 2025 because I kept seeing the same pattern: plenty of enthusiasm, a pile of trials, and very little in daily use. I work with a few scale-ups at a time, hands-on, alongside the people who do the work.

I test new models and tools as they land and write up what matters for growing companies, so you can see how I think before we ever speak.
