A commission-free marketplace for local services

Gig connects people who need a job done with people who do that work. It is built for services nearby — cleaning, repairs, tutoring, moving, and the rest — and for work that can be done from anywhere. One free profile covers both sides. You hire, or you offer, from the same account.

The number that separates it from a typical services app is the commission: 0%. Gig never sits in the payment. You and the other person agree a price and settle it directly, so what was quoted is what gets kept.

Find local help

Describe the job in your own words. “Fix my leaking sink” is a better search than “plumber.” The closest matches surface ranked by relevance, rating, and distance, priced up front, with no platform markup added on top. Message in the app before you commit. How full the results are depends on who is actually near you.

Frequently asked

What is Gig?

Gig is a free marketplace for local services, and for work that can be done from anywhere. One profile lets you hire or offer a service. You describe the job in a sentence, or you list what you do. Gig takes no commission.

How much commission does Gig take?

Zero. Gig charges 0% commission and never touches the payment. The customer pays you directly, so you keep 100%. There is no booking fee and no charge per lead.

Is Gig free to join?

Yes. Gig is free to download on iOS and Android, and free to create a profile on, whether you are hiring or offering a service. There is no subscription and no per-job fee.

How do you find local help on Gig?

Type the job the way you would say it, not a category name. Matches come back ranked by relevance, rating, and distance, with the price up front and no platform fee added. How many people are near you depends on your area.

Is Gig safe to use?

You can sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Ratings run both ways after a job. Profiles can link to Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, or a website, so you can see someone's work before you meet. Messages stay in the app, and you can block or report at any point. Gig does not run background checks, and it does not hold the payment.
