/ rfcfinder · rugby club directory · live on the web

Find a rugby club, wherever you land.

Enter a location, set a radius, and see every club in range on a map and in a list — with the details you need to actually turn up.

    Open rfcfinder.com iOS Coming soon Android Coming soon
    What it is
    The answer to “is there a rugby club near me?” — a question that today gets answered with a search, a dead Facebook page and a bit of word of mouth. RFC Finder makes it a thirty-second lookup. It’s live now at rfcfinder.com.
    Finding a club
    Type a location and set a radius. Clubs in range appear as pins on the map and as a list underneath, both filtered together by Men’s, Women’s, Mixed, Youth or Old Boys. Open one and you get the full profile — crest, year founded, division and category, practice ground, practice days and times, website, email and phone.
    Being found
    Clubs are seeded by hand so the map is useful from day one. A club can then claim its own profile and keep it current — claims are approved manually, so a listing can’t be hijacked.
    How it’s built
    One Expo codebase targeting web, iOS and Android, on Supabase with PostGIS for the radius search and Mapbox for the map and the location autocomplete. Rugby is the launch vertical, but the club-specific parts are isolated on purpose — the same directory re-skins for any other sport or hobby.
    Status
    The web app is live and open to anyone. The iOS and Android builds come from the same codebase and are on the way — no date promised until they’re through review.
